# Attribution, sources and AI/prior-work disclosure

Do not assume the hackathon permits arbitrary prebuilt work, third-party model weights or external datasets. Check **event-specific** written rules after the reveal. Do not attach a licence to the team's own work without a team decision.

## Record AI assistance accurately

For material generated code, explanations, models or assets: tool/provider and purpose; what was generated vs human-written; human verification and tests; whether event requires a disclosure; source licence and any restrictions. Do **not** commit proprietary prompts, keys, private conversations or personal participant information. Avoid claiming a human manually authored generated code, or that a library/tool is a team's own invention.

## Claims checklist

- A paper result is research, not this team's measured outcome.
- A mock/fake action is documented as mock, simulation as simulation, on-device operation only when observed.
- Dataset provenance, model constraints and consent follow their terms.
- Hardware/sponsor/API credits are used only if authorised and actually available.
- Any third-party repo/asset is evaluated for licence compatibility before copying.

Record concise facts here and detailed code/version in the actual dependency files and commit history.
