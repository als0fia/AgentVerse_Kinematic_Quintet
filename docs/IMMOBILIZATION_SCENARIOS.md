# Rice Paddy Robot Immobilization ("Stuck") Scenarios & Multi-Agent Recovery Matrix

**Scope Note:** This document catalogs the primary physical failure and immobilization modes in flooded and drained Southern Chinese rice paddies (*Lannitian* / 烂泥田 and terraced *Bazhangtian* / 巴掌田), how the **Antenna** multi-sensor fusion stack detects and diagnoses each scenario without hidden world-truth leakage, and how the AI Coordinator evaluates recovery vs. task reallocation trade-offs. See [ANTENNA_RF_SUBSYSTEM](ANTENNA_RF_SUBSYSTEM.md), [ARCHITECTURE](ARCHITECTURE.md), and [COMMERCIALIZATION_CHINA](COMMERCIALIZATION_CHINA.md).

## 1. Summary Matrix of Rice Field Immobilization Scenarios

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S1** | **Plow-Pan Breach & Belly-Out ("陷车" / Deep Mud Sinkage)** | Localized weak/broken hardpan (*Lannitian* mud pocket $>30\text{ cm}$ deep); chassis belly contacts cohesive clay mud, unloading wheel normal force. | **Wheel encoders** report high angular velocity ($\omega > 0$), **mmWave Doppler radar (BGT60TR13C)** reports near-zero true ground speed ($v_{\text{true}} \approx 0$), **UWB DS-TWR** range to peers $\Delta r_{ij} \approx 0$, and **IMU** detects static sinkage pitch/roll offset. | **Abort self-spin immediately** (spinning digs deeper ruts). | **S2** | **Differential / Asymmetric Mud Trap (Yaw Lock & Pivot Spin)** | Left wheels enter a soft mud pocket or submerged drainage furrow while right wheels remain on firm soil, causing severe asymmetric drag. | **IMU (ICM-42688-P)** gyroscope measures uncommanded yaw rate ($\dot{\psi}_{\text{meas}} \neq \dot{\psi}_{\text{cmd}}$); **Dual-Antenna UWB PDoA (AoA)** bearing shifts without forward translation; asymmetric motor phase current ($I_L \neq I_R$). | Execute bounded reverse-arc self-recovery backing out along the verified incoming trajectory. If second attempt fails, hold position and reroute peer robots around the asymmetric hazard boundary. |
| **S3** | **Earthen Bund / Terrace Ridge High-Centering ("搁浅" / Straddling a Bund)** | Crossing a narrow ($30\text{--}50\text{ cm}$) slippery field ridge (*田埂*) or submerged levee where the chassis center bottoms out on the crest, lifting drive wheels off the ground. | **IMU** detects sharp pitch transition ($\theta > 15^\circ \rightarrow 0^\circ$) with elevated roll instability; **Wheel encoders** spin freely with **low motor current** (wheels unloaded in water/air) while **mmWave radar** & **UWB** confirm $v_{\text{true}} = 0$. | Self-recovery is impossible once wheels lose ground contact. Coordinator dispatches a partner unit on the lower/upper terrace for a **short-impulse tug via tow hitch** (monitored by **INA240 strain gauge**), or flags human operator assist. |
| **S4** | **Submerged Irrigation Channel / Drainage Trench Drop-In** | One or two front/side wheels drop into a turbid, water-filled perimeter drainage ditch (*排水沟*, $20\text{--}35\text{ cm}$ deep) invisible to optical cameras due to muddy water surface reflection. | Sudden step change in **IMU roll/pitch** ($>18^\circ$) + vertical Z-acceleration spike; **ToF (VL53L8CX)** range discontinuity; **UWB PDoA** tilt-compensated projection flags attitude limit exceedance. | Trigger immediate deterministic safety `HOLD` to prevent rollover into water. Mark trench vector on shared map; require **angled multi-robot tether recovery** away from the ditch edge. |
| **S5** | **Rice Straw Mat / Green Manure & Root Entanglement** | Un-decomposed previous-season rice straw (*秸秆还田*), filamentous algae, or Tough weeds wrap around wheel hubs, axles, or the seed-release dropper chute. | **Motor current ($I_{\text{motor}}$) spikes near stall threshold** while **wheel encoder velocity drops ($\omega \rightarrow 0$)** and **mmWave radar** shows $v_{\text{true}} \rightarrow 0$ (distinguished from mud slip where $\omega$ is high). | Execute short reverse-forward clearing oscillation to shed straw. If hub torque remains stalled, disable propulsion on that unit, convert it into a **stationary UWB/Wi-Fi HaLow relay beacon**, and reallocate its planting queue. |
| **S6** | **Suction Lock During Stationary Seed Drop / Probing** | While paused ($v = 0$) in thixotropic clay mud to insert a soil probe or drop a seed proxy, cohesive mud settles and creates vacuum suction around the wheels/belly. | Upon issuing a post-plant `DRIVE` command, both **motor current** and **wheel slip** surge simultaneously while **UWB + mmWave radar** show zero initial displacement. | Apply gradual micro-rocking torque profile (alternating $\pm$ low-duty-cycle pulses to break mud thixotropy without shearing the soil crust) before resuming normal linear velocity. |
| **S7** | **Tether Snag / Cooperative Tow Overload** | During tethered formation travel or an active rescue pull, the physical cable snags on a field corner post, rock, or dense crop hill, or the stuck robot's mud resistance exceeds safe pull limits. | **Tow Hitch Strain Gauge (INA240)** measures tension spike ($F_{\text{pull}} > 350\text{--}400\text{ N}$) while **UWB inter-robot distance ($r_{ij}$)** remains static and rescue robot's **mmWave radar** detects incipient wheel slip on the rescue unit itself. | **Deterministic safety gate aborts pull immediately** to prevent trapping the second robot or uprooting planted rice rows. Coordinator rejects direct line-of-sight tow and either plans a pulley/detour angle or defers to human recovery. |
| **S8** | **Canopy Multipath / Narrow Furrow Pinch ("夾行")** | Navigating between established rice hills where dense wet foliage causes GNSS/UWB multipath jumps, or a collapsing mud wall pinches the robot against living rice plants. | **EKF Innovation Gate** rejects sudden UWB range jumps ($\Delta r_{ij}$) that violate the **mmWave radar + odometry dead-reckoning envelope**; **Whiskers + ToF (VL53L8CX)** detect bilateral zero-clearance ($e_y \rightarrow 0$ on both sides). | Switch navigation authority 100% to local **Whisker + ToF furrow-lock controller** at reduced speed; if bilateral pinch persists, back out along recorded odometry breadcrumb trail rather than turning in-place (which would crush adjacent rice hills). |

## 2. Diagnostic Decision Tree: Distinguishing "Stuck" Modes via Sensor Fusion

A key engineering advantage of the **Antenna** architecture is that it does **not** treat "robot stopped moving" as a single generic fault. By cross-referencing four onboard physical signals at 200 Hz on the STM32H7, the robot classifies the exact immobilization physics before consulting the high-level AI Coordinator:

$$\text{Slip Ratio } s = 1 - \frac{v_{\text{radar}}}{r_{\text{wheel}} \cdot \omega_{\text{encoder}}}$$

1. **High Wheel Speed ($\omega \uparrow$) + Zero Ground Speed ($v_{\text{radar}} \approx 0, \Delta r_{\text{UWB}} \approx 0$) + Normal/High Current ($I \uparrow$):**
   $\rightarrow$ **Cohesive Mud Spin (Scenario S1 / S2).** *Rule:* Stop spinning immediately; wheel rotation excavates mud beneath the chassis and worsens sinkage.
2. **High Wheel Speed ($\omega \uparrow$) + Zero Ground Speed ($v_{\text{radar}} \approx 0$) + Unusually Low Current ($I \downarrow$) + Pitch/Roll Offset:**
   $\rightarrow$ **High-Centered on Bund / Trench Drop (Scenario S3 / S4).** *Rule:* Wheels are suspended in air/water; only external tether force can recover the unit.
3. **Zero Wheel Speed ($\omega \approx 0$) + Maximum Stall Current ($I \rightarrow I_{\max}$) + Zero Ground Speed ($v_{\text{radar}} \approx 0$):**
   $\rightarrow$ **Mechanical Straw/Root Entanglement or Suction Lock (Scenario S5 / S6).** *Rule:* Attempt brief reverse-polarity clearing pulse; if still stalled, shut down drive stage to prevent MOSFET/motor thermal burnout.
4. **Rising Hitch Tension ($F_{\text{pull}} \rightarrow 400\text{ N}$) + Rescue Robot Slip Ratio Rising ($s_{\text{rescue}} > 0.4$) + Constant UWB Distance ($\dot{r}_{ij} = 0$):**
   $\rightarrow$ **Cascading Rescue Failure (Scenario S7).** *Rule:* Abort rescue immediately so a single stuck robot does not turn into two stuck robots.

## 3. AI Coordinator Multi-Objective Rescue Evaluation

When a robot reports an immobilization state (`S1`–`S8`), the central/distributed **Antenna** AI Coordinator evaluates five candidate actions against an explicit cost-benefit objective:

1. **Bounded Self-Recovery (`SELF_RECOVER`):** Allowed only for `S2` (reverse-arc), `S5` (reverse de-tangle pulse), or `S6` (thixotropic micro-rocking), capped at a strict energy and time budget ($\le 5\text{ s}$) to prevent soil structure destruction.
2. **Direct Tethered Rescue (`DISPATCH_RESCUE_DIRECT`):** Dispatches a nearby unit if an unplanted, firm-soil corridor exists and expected pull tension $F_{\text{pull}} < 300\text{ N}$.
3. **Detour / Perimeter Rescue (`DISPATCH_RESCUE_DETOUR`):** Selected when the shortest line between the rescue robot and the stuck robot crosses **already-planted seedlings** or a soft-mud zone. The coordinator trades higher travel time/energy for **zero crop trampling damage**.
4. **Role Conversion & Task Reallocation (`REALLOCATE_AND_BEACON`):** If the immobilized robot still has battery and functional **Qorvo DWM3000 UWB + Wi-Fi HaLow** transceivers, it is converted into a **fixed ultra-stable UWB anchor beacon** (improving PDoA/DS-TWR localization precision for the remaining robots) while its unplanted target sites are reassigned to healthy units.
5. **Safe Abstention & Human Escalation (`DEFER_TO_HUMAN`):** Chosen when any rescue attempt has a high probability of secondary immobilization (`S1` deep plow-pan breach surrounded by saturated mud, or `S7` tension overload). Preserves the rest of the swarm and logs exact UWB/BeiDou coordinates for post-mission manual retrieval.
